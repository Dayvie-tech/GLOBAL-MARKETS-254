How it works

A clear four-stage process: create an account, complete verification, review a campaign brief, then submit genuine evidence for review.

01 · Create an account

Register with accurate contact details and keep your login credentials private.

02 · Complete verification

Submit the required registration payment reference. An administrator reviews it; payment submission alone does not activate an account.

03 · Choose a campaign

Review the campaign's instructions, approved media, rate if applicable, deadline and eligibility conditions before starting.

04 · Submit genuine proof

Complete the task honestly and upload the requested evidence before the campaign closes. A human review determines approval.

Important

There is no guaranteed income, view count or approval. Each campaign has its own conditions and review process.
