Terms of participation

The basic expectations for accounts, campaigns, proof submissions and withdrawals. Campaign-specific conditions also apply.

Account accuracy

Provide accurate registration details and protect your login credentials. Accounts may be restricted for misuse or false information.

Campaign participation

Read the full brief before participating. Use only authorised media and follow the platform, timing, content and evidence requirements.

Genuine activity

Fake views, engagement, screenshots, identities or manipulated evidence are prohibited. Submissions are subject to review and may be rejected.

Compensation

Rates and eligibility are campaign-specific. Pending submissions are not approved earnings. No income or view count is guaranteed. The current minimum withdrawal request is KSh 1,000 and requests are subject to balance checks and processing review.

Account action

Accounts may be suspended where there is suspected fraud, abuse or a material breach of campaign rules. Members should use the support process to raise a concern.
